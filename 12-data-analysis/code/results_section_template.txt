# Results — section skeleton

Drop this into your `acmart`/`IEEEtran` draft and replace the brackets. Rule:
**lead with the effect, carry the interval, let the p-value bring up the rear.**
No interpretation here — that is the Discussion (Chapter 13). Results = what you
measured, how big, how sure, what test. Nothing more.

---
