# The Heilmeier Catechism — Worksheet

George Heilmeier's questions for any research program. The sharpest "so what?"
filter ever written. Answer for each top candidate question. Be honest on #2 and #4 —
those are where weak questions die.

> Rule: answer #1 with NO jargon. If you can't, your question is foggy.
> Answer #2 with a CITATION. If you can't, your gap is `unconfirmed`.
