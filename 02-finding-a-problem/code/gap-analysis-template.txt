# Gap-Analysis Template (CARS) — Research Opportunity Report

Fill one of these for each top candidate. The three moves are Swales' CARS model:
establish the **territory**, carve the **niche** (the cited gap), then **occupy** it.
Every claim gets a citation OR an honest `unconfirmed` tag — no exceptions.
