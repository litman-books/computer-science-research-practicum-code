# Annotation — TEMPLATE (one per source, 100–200 words)

Write this in the Zotero item's Notes field WHILE the paper is open.
An annotation written from memory drifts from what the paper actually says.

Five parts. The fourth (relevance) is the one beginners skip and reviewers live for.

---

## [Citation — full, correct, VERIFIED in dblp / Semantic Scholar]

Author(s). "Title." Venue, Year. (DOI / arXiv id)

**1. Summary (your own words):**
The problem, the method, the key result — one or two sentences. If you can't
summarize it, you haven't read it.

**2. Method & evidence:**
What did they actually DO? Dataset/benchmark, baseline(s), metric(s), and the
headline number. (These become columns in your Chapter 4 comparison matrix.)

**3. Relevance to MY project:**  <-- the most important line
Why is this in my bibliography? Is it my baseline? A method I'll adapt? The gap
I'm filling? The nearest competitor I must differentiate from?

**4. Limitation / my critique:**
What did they NOT address? Narrow benchmark? No error bars? Untested transfer to
my domain? This is often where MY opening is.

**5. Verification:** dblp ✓  Semantic Scholar ✓  read in full ✓   (remove #unverified tag)

## WORKED EXAMPLE

## Jay, N., et al. "A Deep Reinforcement Learning Perspective on Internet Congestion Control." ICML 2019.

**1. Summary:** Proposes Aurora, an RL agent that learns a congestion-control
policy from experience rather than hand-tuned heuristics, and shows it can match
or beat classic schemes on several network conditions.

**2. Method & evidence:** RL (policy gradient) over a network simulator; baselines
include TCP CUBIC and BBR; metric is a reward combining throughput, latency, and
loss. Reports competitive performance across simulated link conditions.

**3. Relevance to my project:** This is the canonical applied-AI anchor for my
networking domain and my likely baseline — my contribution must differentiate
from learned congestion control, not just from classic TCP.

**4. Limitation / my critique:** Evaluated largely in simulation; sim-to-real
transfer and stability under adversarial traffic are under-tested — a candidate
gap for my work.

**5. Verification:** dblp ✓  Semantic Scholar ✓  read in full ✓
