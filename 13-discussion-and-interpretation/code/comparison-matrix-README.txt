How to use comparison-matrix.csv (instructions kept out of the CSV so it loads cleanly in pandas, Excel and Sheets)

Fill the first three rows with YOUR real prior-work papers. Replace the YOUR WORK row with your numbers.
Read DOWN each column: an empty/unanswered column is a gap. Compare your result row against the priors.
Watch for ASYMMETRY: a prior single-run number is NOT comparable to your multi-seed mean (Bouthillier 2021).
Verify every prior-work number at its source (dblp / Semantic Scholar) before you cite it in the Discussion.
