# Claim–Evidence Map (hand version of the widget)

Break your draft Discussion into **atomic claims** — one assertion per row. For each, name the
*specific* evidence in your paper that licenses it (a table cell, a figure, a test — not "the
experiments"). Then assign exactly one verdict.

**Verdicts**
- **Supported** — a result in your paper directly licenses this exact claim.
- **Over-reach** — the result points this way but the claim says more than the result earns
  (wrong scope, missing condition, causal language over correlational evidence). → *Narrow it.*
- **Unsupported** — no result in the paper backs this claim at all. → *Measure it (move to Results)
  or delete it.* No Unsupported claim may survive to submission.
