# Point-by-Point Response (Rebuttal) Skeleton

A rebuttal answers reviewers; it does not argue with them. Civil, specific, evidence-first.
NeurIPS 2026: each response is capped at 10,000 characters per review, plain text +
OpenReview markdown, NO file uploads (only an anonymized code link if a reviewer asked).
New results are allowed, but the ORIGINAL submission stays the basis for the decision —
so promise changes you can actually make.

Re-verify the exact limit for YOUR target venue/year before relying on a number here.

---

## Global response (one short paragraph, all reviewers)
Thank the reviewers. State the 2–3 most important changes you made across the board.
"We thank the reviewers for their careful reading. In response we (1) added a multi-seed
table with 95% CIs (R1, R3), (2) corrected the baseline in §4.2 (R2), and (3) clarified
the data split (R1)."

## Per-reviewer, per-comment
Quote or paraphrase the comment, then respond. Use a stable tag so reviewers can find it.

### Reviewer 1
> **R1.1 (MAJOR):** "Results are single-seed; the gain may be noise."
**Response — Done.** We reran across 10 seeds (Table 3, revised). The mean gain is
+2.4 points (95% CI [1.1, 3.7]); ASO with τ=0.2 vs. the baseline confirms stochastic
dominance. Text updated in §5.1.

> **R1.2 (CLARIFY):** "Was scaling fit on train only?"
**Response — Clarified.** Yes. The scaler is fit on the training fold only, inside the
CV loop, to avoid leakage (Kapoor & Narayanan 2023). We made this explicit in §4.1.

### Reviewer 2
> **R2.1 (MINOR):** "Typo in Eq. 3 / Figure 2 caption unclear."
**Response — Fixed.** Corrected; caption now stands alone.

> **R2.2 (DISAGREE — handle with care):** "The contribution is incremental."
**Response — Respectfully clarified.** We see the contribution as X, which prior work
[7, 12] did not address because Y; we added a sentence to the intro making the
delta explicit. We do not claim a new architecture; we claim a measured result. We have
revised the claim language to match what the evidence supports.

---

## Three rules
1. Lead with the verb: **Done / Fixed / Clarified / Added / Respectfully clarified.**
2. Never concede a result you didn't actually change, and never claim a change you can't make.
3. If a reviewer is simply wrong, show the evidence calmly — don't get the last word, get the right one.
