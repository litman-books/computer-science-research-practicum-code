## Decision rule (the practicum default)

A 16-week practicum draft is **work-in-progress**. The honest first target is a
**workshop or Student Research Workshop short/WIP paper (~2-4 pages)** — an
on-ramp, not the main conference. Workshops have higher acceptance rates and
exist precisely for student and early-stage work. Pipeline: workshop -> full
conference -> extended journal version.

Note (as of 2026): a workshop-reviewed survey/position paper does NOT clear the
arXiv-CS bar. arXiv (Oct 31, 2025) requires CS review/survey/position papers to
already be peer-review-accepted at a journal or conference, with a DOI; workshop
review is explicitly insufficient. A separate Jan 21, 2026 endorsement change
means an institutional email alone no longer qualifies a new submitter. Plan the
preprint step accordingly; verify both rules live before you rely on them.
